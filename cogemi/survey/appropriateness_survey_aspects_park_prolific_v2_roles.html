Social Appropriateness Study

You will read ~47 short vignettes set in a public park. For each one you will be asked four quick questions: how appropriate you find the action, what you think others would consider appropriate, how common the behaviour is, and how certain you are of your judgement. You will then identify which aspect of the situation most influenced you.

The study takes approximately 20–25 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers.

Your data: Responses are stored securely and linked only to your Prolific ID, which will be removed from the analysis dataset after payment. We also record the type of device you are using and the width of your browser window, so that we can check whether the layout affected your answers. Participation is voluntary; you may withdraw at any time.

This research is conducted by ISIR, Sorbonne Université. If you have questions, contact tjostheim@isir.upmc.fr.
